First: how to write your answers

1-mark literature answer: One complete sentence, naming the exact person or fact. 2-mark answer: Two clear points, usually two sentences. 3–5 marks: Start with a direct answer, add two or three relevant details from the chapter, finish with the meaning/theme if asked. For a poem, explain the image as well as what it suggests. Do not retell the whole chapter for a short question.

Essay format (no fixed decorative heading required): Write the topic as a title. Paragraph 1: introduce your idea. Paragraphs 2–3: two distinct points, with an example. Final paragraph: a balanced conclusion. Keep to the word limit on your actual paper; the model essays below are practice, not an official word count. Avoid unsupported facts, long quotations and memorised essays that do not answer the prompt. Harmony itself prescribes introduction → body → conclusion and says essay questions can draw on literature/culture, science/technology, values, agriculture/economy, sports or travel (book PDF pp. 105–107).

Conversation comprehension format: You are normally given a conversation to read, not asked to compose one. Read the speakers and setting first; locate each answer in their actual words. Number your answers, write complete sentences and copy vocabulary answers exactly from the passage. Harmony advises against adding things not present in the dialogue (PDF pp. 121–123).

Grammar answer format: For question tags, rewrite the statement and append the tag after a comma: “She is ready, isn't she?” For common errors, rewrite the whole correct sentence, not just the changed word. Positive statement → negative tag; negative statement or negative idea (few, seldom, nobody) → positive tag. “Let's” → “shall we?” Requests → “will you?” (Harmony PDF pp. 111–116).

The paper pattern: what is known, what is not

The 2025 and 2026 final board papers are 100 marks in three hours: prose 40, poetry 30, grammar 10 (five question tags + five corrected sentences), and writing/comprehension 20 (one essay from four topics, 10; one conversation passage, 10). The district half-yearly paper tomorrow is two hours. Do not assume the full-board weights, number of options or essay length carry over to it; follow the printed question paper. The 2025 essay choices included Women Empowerment, Knowledge is Power, Global Warming and the Future of Mankind, and My Dream Holiday Destination; 2026 choices included Sports as a Career in India, Youth Empowerment, India's North-East as a Tourism Destination, and The Importance of Ecological Conservation. These show broad themes, not a reliable prediction for tomorrow.

Revision route tonight: read five summaries → test yourself with the short answers and MCQs → write one essay from memory → do both grammar sets → attempt one unseen conversation. Rest before the morning exam.
