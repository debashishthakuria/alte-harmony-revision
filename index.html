How to write answers

1 mark: One complete sentence with the exact fact.

2 marks: Two clear points in two sentences.

3–5 marks: Answer directly, add details from the chapter, then explain the meaning if asked.

Comprehension: Read the passage and answer from what the speakers actually say.
